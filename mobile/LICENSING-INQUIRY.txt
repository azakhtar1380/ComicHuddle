Subject: ComicHuddle commercial data partnership — web, iOS and Android

Hello,

I am building ComicHuddle, a comic collector marketplace and community with personal collections, issue discovery, value tracking, messaging and comic pressing services. The initial release will include responsive desktop/mobile web and native iOS/Android apps.

I would like a commercial licensing quote for your data. My initial target is $250–500 per month, subject to your minimums and coverage. Please quote an early-stage pilot and explain how pricing scales with users, request volume and displayed records.

Please confirm:

1. Catalog scope: issues, publishers, series, variants, printings, editions, languages, stable identifiers and the number of records actually covered. How are new releases and corrections delivered?
2. Raw and graded valuations: raw condition buckets, CGC numerical grades, label categories, signed/restored/qualified/conserved copies, currencies, estimate methodology, confidence/sample counts and update frequency. Identify categories you do not value.
3. Display rights: use in free and paid accounts, collection totals, alerts, listing comparisons, public collector showcases, web, iOS and Android. May we display sale histories and individual comparable-sale facts?
4. Cover images: are cover images included, and do you have the right to sublicense public commercial display across these platforms? Please identify rights limitations, required credits, territories, thumbnail/full-size restrictions and whether linking differs from hosting.
5. Delivery: documented API or licensed file feed, sandbox, bulk bootstrap, deltas/webhooks, rate limits, permitted server caching, retention, SLA and support.
6. Mapping: documentation for your issue/variant identifiers and any permitted crosswalk to Grand Comics Database records or a separate canonical catalog.
7. Legal terms: redistribution permission, attribution, usage reporting, contract duration, renewal pricing, termination/expiry behavior, data deletion and responsibility for underlying third-party rights. We will not scrape a consumer price guide or ship your keys in a client app.

If the requested coverage exceeds the initial budget, please propose a narrower pilot with a path to broader coverage. Please distinguish commercial redistribution rights from an individual subscription and send sample data plus the proposed agreement before any purchase.

Thank you,
ComicHuddle founder
