## Key Collector pricing versus an original model

The official FAQ describes averaging up to ten ungraded secondary-market sales within one month, then reducing approximately 10–15% into LOW/MID/HIGH acquisition ranges. Those buckets are explicitly not numeric professional grades. That discount is buying guidance, not a required component of our FMV. [2]

**Important current product update:** Key Collector's publisher description in Apple's App Store separately advertises Graded Pricing. Do not claim it has no graded product. Its commercial grade coverage, feed availability and methodology remain unconfirmed, and the raw FAQ cannot supply those answers. [4]

Our independently specified prototype uses an exact-edition/condition/label cohort, verified settled sales, a recency-weighted median and an observed middle-price range. Its proposed evidence gates and uncalibrated thresholds are documented in [INDEPENDENT-FMV-METHOD.md](INDEPENDENT-FMV-METHOD.md). It applies no automatic buying discount. It must disclose dates, retained sample count, price basis and support; sparse evidence produces an unavailable result. This is a research estimate until backtesting supports stronger claims, not a reconstruction of another guide.

Potential inputs include permission-cleared external feeds, dealers' original consented sale ledgers and eventually ComicHuddle's real settled transactions. Third-party database exports retain their restrictions; uploading one does not clear its rights. Private preview listings, accepted offers without settlement and test auctions are not completed sales.

## What eBay can and cannot supply today

**Browse** supports finding purchasable listings and their item details, including fixed-price and auction options. Those are asks/current bids, not a general completed-sales valuation feed. Its guide says not to pull in listings whose end date is in the past. [5]

**Marketplace Insights:** eBay's current marketplace-support page explicitly says it is restricted and not open to new users. Its overview redirects to private documentation sign-in. A normal developer account cannot be treated as an available sold-history license; ask whether any approved alternative exists. [6, 7]

**Product Research/Seller Hub:** official help describes three years of sales and actual accepted Best Offer amounts; ordinary completed search covers recently sold listings over 90 days. This is a seller research product, not evidence that Malik LLC may automate, export or redistribute its database. An ended listing or displayed original ask is not proof of an actual accepted, paid price. eBay's User Agreement requires prior express permission for automated gathering and commercialization. [8, 9]

**Standard API constraints:** §§8.1 and 8.5 require purpose-limited copies, removal of no-longer-public content, visual separation from non-eBay material, and listing freshness within six hours (other content: 24 hours). These are not blanket archive-retention rights. Restricted-data pricing tools need express written consent; approved tools/outputs grant rights back to eBay. §9 also restricts competing-marketplace use and price modeling without written consent; restricted-data bulk/API redistribution has additional limits. Thus ordinary API approval does not clear ComicHuddle FMV. Listing photos are not an evergreen catalog-art license. [10]

### Concrete eBay access steps

1. Decide whether the integration is external shopping discovery, affiliate referrals or a commercial sold-data/valuation partnership. Disclose ComicHuddle's own marketplace and estimator; do not describe it only as a shopping widget.
2. The published buying-app process starts with an eBay member/developer account, applicable agreements, a reviewable integration/Sandbox tests and an Application Growth Check where required. The production requirements direct partners through EPN, a Buy API application, business-model review, Developer Support and contracts. Affiliate payments need approved EPN participation; approval is not guaranteed. Verify which steps eBay applies to this use before substantial integration spending. [11, 12]
3. Ask Developer Technical Support through the official buying-app guide whether a sold-comparable feed is offered to a new partner and what agreement would authorize our requested calculation/display. Do not use the closed Insights service, private login traffic or public sold-page scraping as a workaround.
4. Obtain scope for exact accepted prices, delivery/settlement/refunds, identity/grade fields, lookback, updates, licensed derivation, public/paid/native outputs, combinations with first-party records, retention, images, exports and deletion. An inaccessible or undocumented field must stay unknown.
5. If approved, isolate eBay discovery results from our marketplace, keep secrets server-side, enforce contracted freshness/rights removal, and reconcile supplied samples. If declined, use independent permitted sale records or a vendor's licensed valuations.
