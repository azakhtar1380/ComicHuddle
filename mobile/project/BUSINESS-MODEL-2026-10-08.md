# ComicHuddle: proposed revenue model

The business combines collection identity, mutual trade discovery, comic commerce and trusted comic care providers. The directory attracts searches; profiles and groups retain collectors; completed transactions and useful professional tools earn revenue. A large licensed catalog alone is not a defensible business.

These prices are proposals, not activated plans or a forecast. The preview has no connected checkout and takes no fees.

| Offer | Proposed price | What earns the payment |
|---|---:|---|
| Free collector account | $0 | Collection, showcase, wants, groups, messages, listing basics and mutual matching |
| Collector Plus | $7.99/month | Licensed historical valuation tools, configurable alerts, advanced collection analytics and exports, if the contract permits these uses |
| Marketplace fee | 7% of completed item sales | Seller onboarding, checkout, order tracking and dispute handling once implemented; disclose actual payment fees separately |
| Seller Pro | $29/month | Bulk listing tools, inventory import, storefront customization and reporting |
| Pressing Studio Pro | $19/month | Intake forms, quote templates, scheduling and service workflow tools |

Keep basic provider discovery available without a subscription. Paid tools must not buy positive reviews or organic ranking. Clearly label any later sponsorship. Start with original brand merchandise or separately authorized merchandise; a comic-data license does not license Marvel/DC character products.

## Illustrative monthly target

| Revenue stream | Assumption | Gross platform revenue |
|---|---:|---:|
| Collector Plus | 500 × $7.99 | $3,995 |
| Seller Pro | 50 × $29 | $1,450 |
| Completed marketplace fees | $75,000 item sales × 7% | $5,250 |
| Pressing Studio Pro | 25 × $19 | $475 |
| Total | Hypothetical scenario | $11,170 |

The $75,000 belongs to the sellers before their fees and payouts; it is not ComicHuddle revenue. A $375 assumed monthly data cost and $3,000 other-cost allowance leave $7,795 before tax, but this is a simple planning remainder, not an audited profit estimate. The allowance must cover the actual processor/Connect fees, app-store commissions where applicable, hosting, customer acquisition, support, refunds and other operating costs. If those exceed the allowance, the remainder falls. Provider pricing and all conversion assumptions are unconfirmed.

At $7.99, 47 subscriptions cover a $375 data bill before payment fees and all other expenses. At a 7% fee, about $5,358 in completed sales covers that same bill before other costs. That is only data-cost coverage, not business break-even.

For context, 500 paying collectors at a hypothetical 5% conversion rate require 10,000 active eligible collectors. Neither the audience nor the conversion is guaranteed. Measure actual retention, paid conversion, completed sales, contribution margin after transaction costs and repeat use before adding fixed expenses.

## First revenue sequence

1. Inventory your own genuine comics and recruit approximately ten dependable dealers and five pressing studios. These are recruitment targets, not existing partners.
2. Make exact edition/condition descriptions and seller conversations work reliably; grow groups around specific collecting interests.
3. Connect supported marketplace payments, seller verification and payout controls. Publish clear fees, condition/photo requirements and dispute/return procedures before taking money.
4. License data and introduce Plus only when its promised data features exist and the license permits them.
5. Offer Seller/Studio Pro when their workflow tools save operators measurable time. Validate willingness to pay with real operators.

Physical comics and real-world pressing services follow the stores' rules for physical transactions. Digital premium app tools have different billing rules, with regional exceptions; plan compliant store billing or permitted alternatives before a native release. Do not assume that web Stripe checkout universally satisfies mobile digital-subscription rules.
