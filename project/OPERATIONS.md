# ComicHuddle operational handoff

This is a proposed runbook for the founder to complete before inviting public user content. It does not claim staffed support, backup coverage, payment protection or guaranteed legal clearance.

## Moderation and account controls

The owner opens Account > Settings > Review community reports. Review the reported resource and concern. Removing a profile/provider applies a durable account/provider restriction, hides its discovery and disables new activity. Fixed-price listings are withdrawn and pending offers closed; auction previews are cancelled and active proposals annulled while audit history remains. Existing private custody records remain available to their authorized collector/provider where needed for safe completion.

Active restrictions appear in the same owner review screen. Review the concern before restoring permissions. Restoring leaves the profile hidden until its owner explicitly publishes it. Document a public appeal/support route and retention basis before wider launch.

Member reports may target content they can actually access. Moderation restrictions remain after a public profile is deleted to prevent immediate evasion. Minimal restriction data and anonymized auction history remain under the disclosed preview rules; final retention periods need review. Restriction data remains until owner review; counter rows expire and are removed opportunistically by later authenticated writes. Final retention periods and security-log/backup treatment must be published by the operator.

These are supplied details, not evidence of independently verified mailbox staffing, company status, assumed-name filing, insurance or legal clearance.

Account > Settings > Auction previews opens the owner-only support screen. It shows the latest 100 previews, including hidden and ended records. Use the exact listing ID supplied in a support request to find an older record. Review the concern and use the confirmation checkbox before cancellation. Cancellation is a server-controlled transaction that releases copy reservations and annuls active proposals; accepted amounts/timestamps remain as audit history. No preview creates a payment or order. Report removal also cancels the affected auction.

Sellers may withdraw a preview with no accepted proposals. Any accepted bid history requires owner cancellation before seller account deletion, including ended or retracted proposals. Bidders must retract their latest still-open unretracted proposals before deletion; repeated retraction may be needed for earlier unretracted proposals. Closed/cancelled bidder history is anonymized on deletion. Cancellation and retention/appeal procedures need a final published operating policy before wider use.

## Data access and closure

Users can export their collection separately or use Export all my account records. The full JSON contains private records, rule receipt and conversations/care evidence; users should keep it private. This is a personal portability export, not a complete backup or restore service.

Account deletion removes the application's owned records and uploads, preserving minimal abuse-prevention holds. Active accepted care requests can prevent deletion while custody records are needed. Before public use, define a closure/support process for stuck requests: immediately hide discovery/disable general messages when appropriate, preserve the minimum necessary shipment evidence, resolve returns/claims, and disclose retained records. Deletion does not remove the independent ChatGPT identity.

## Incident and recovery preparation

Assign a named support/moderation owner, working contact, report-review cadence and escalation backup. Set privacy-conscious error monitoring, avoiding message bodies, home addresses and photos in logs. Confirm hosted database/media backup capabilities and demonstrate restore of representative records/media in an isolated environment. Record restore evidence and recovery targets after measurement, rather than promising arbitrary uptime.

On a confirmed privacy/data-integrity failure, stop the affected feature or narrow audience; preserve minimal diagnostic evidence, remediate, independently retest, and coordinate any required notices with the operator. Database migrations add fields/tables; rolling back code alone does not undo database state.

## Data rights and commerce

Keep provider credentials and private license references server-side. Contract samples must cover exact editions, grades/labels, source dates, coverage, free/paid web/native redistribution and image display. Expired or stale permissions must remove current-value display. Historical sale facts remain dated and are not substitute current FMV.

Live checkout and binding auction settlement remain unavailable. Test-only commerce routes and order/refund storage are now implemented; the deployed sandbox is disabled without business credentials and reviewed webhook ingress. Auction previews use explicit nonbinding consent and server cutoffs. Applicable auction operating requirements need independent review before public binding bidding. Offers and care requests are expressions/records of interest and participant agreements, not paid orders, escrow, insured custody or guaranteed grade improvement. Connect actual seller verification, server-authoritative orders/payments, refunds/disputes, taxes and a reconciled ledger before taking money. Treat merchant fees, acquisition/support, licensing, disputes and operating costs separately when measuring contribution.

Uncertain processor responses must be reconciled before creating replacement requests. Unknown operations stop retrying after23 hours; a known processor object can be retrieved.

Seller-entered tracking is shared but unverified. No automatic delivery payout or scheduled refund guarantee is operating. Connect trusted parcel/destination/signature checks and a durable scheduler before advertising those protections. Finalize refund/return evidence, custody-aware inventory release, dispute handling, reserves, taxes and legal/provider review. See STRIPE-SANDBOX-SETUP.md and BUYER-PROTECTION-AND-PLATFORM.md.
